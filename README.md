# Managing Devices in Microsoft Intune — A Practical Guide for SMBs
Last updated: 10/01/2026

Part of the **Microsoft 365 Security Foundations** series — practical, production-tested guides for securing Microsoft 365 as a small or growing business.

A hands-on guide to enrolling and securing devices across Windows, Linux, and iOS using Microsoft Intune, written for small and medium businesses managing a mixed fleet of personal and corporate-owned devices.

This covers real enrollment paths and real constraints — including what happens when a device runs an edition that doesn't support full compliance management,and how to handle that honestly instead of pretending it isn't a limitation.

**Disclaimer:** Due to the nature of Intune and Defender's integration, some device management configurations can be found at the Defender repository of this series. See also: [Intune Device Management Guide](https://github.com/LeventKaraagac/microsoft-defender-threat-protection-guide).

## What's covered

1. [Device Enrollment](./01-device-enrollment/) — Linux, iOS (corporate and BYOD), Windows BYOD (documentation pending)
2. [Compliance Policies](./02-compliance-policies/) — including Windows Home-edition constraints (documentation pending)
3. [App Protection Policies](./03-app-protection-policies/) *(documentation pending)*

## Who this is for

Anyone managing a small, mixed device fleet — a mix of corporate-owned and personal devices across different operating systems — who needs a working compliance baseline without assuming every device is a fully managed, enterprise-edition machine.
